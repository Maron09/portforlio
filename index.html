About

M

I'm Chimaroke, but most people call me Maro. I'm a backend developer in Lagos, and I'm drawn to the parts of software where correctness matters: payments, accounts, approvals and audit trails.

My strongest work has come from building alongside the people who use it. I built a digital account-opening system for banks with input from real bank staff, so the workflows follow how branches actually operate.

I also build automation tools for clients, from data scrapers to pipelines that take over repetitive tasks, and I'm currently deepening my C#/.NET skills with larger architecture projects.
